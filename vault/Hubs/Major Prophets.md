# Major Prophets

Part of the [[Old Testament]] · Center: [[Jesus Christ]]

Isaiah through Daniel — 'major' by length, not importance. The Suffering Servant, the new covenant, the new heart, the Son of man on the clouds.

## Books
- [[Isaiah]] — 66 chapters
- [[Jeremiah]] — 52 chapters
- [[Lamentations]] — 5 chapters
- [[Ezekiel]] — 48 chapters
- [[Daniel]] — 12 chapters
