# Gospels

Part of the [[New Testament]] · Center: [[Jesus Christ]]

Four portraits, one Christ: King (Matthew), Servant (Mark), Son of man (Luke), Son of God (John). All four run to a Cross and an empty tomb.

## Books
- [[Matthew]] — 28 chapters
- [[Mark]] — 16 chapters
- [[Luke]] — 24 chapters
- [[John]] — 21 chapters
