# Jesus Christ

> *"And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself."*
> — Luke 24:27 (KJV)

> *"Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me."*
> — John 5:39 (KJV)

**Every book in this vault links here — because every book in the Bible points here.** Open the graph view and you'll see it: sixty-six books, one Person at the center.

## The Cross and the Empty Tomb

The Bible is not a collection of moral tales. It is one story with one hero and one hinge:

> *"For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; And that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures."*
> — 1 Corinthians 15:3–4 (KJV)

**Christ died for our sins** — the Cross is where the guilt of every altar, every lamb, every broken law from Genesis to Malachi was laid on the sinless Substitute.
**He rose again the third day** — the Resurrection is God's public receipt that the payment was accepted. He is alive.

## The Gift

If you're reading this and you've never received Him — this whole vault was built for you:

> *"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."*
> — John 3:16 (KJV)

Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ — that He died for **your** sins and rose again — and call on His name:

> *"For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved."*
> — Romans 10:13 (KJV)

That's it. Not works, not religion, not cleaning yourself up first. **Faith in the crucified, risen Son.** Tell Him now, in your own words.
