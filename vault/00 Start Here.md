# Start Here

Welcome. This vault contains the **complete text of the Bible (KJV — public domain)** as 66 linked notes, built to be read *and* to be seen.

## The Point of the Graph

Open **Graph View** (Ctrl/Cmd + G). You will see [[Jesus Christ]] at the center with all 66 books connected to Him — a visual sermon of Luke 24:27. Around Him, the books connect to each other along real lines of quotation and fulfillment: watch [[Psalms]], [[Isaiah]], and [[Genesis]] light up as the great hubs of Scripture, and see [[Hebrews]] and [[Revelation]] reach back and gather up the whole Old Testament.

**Graph tips:**
- In Graph View settings → **Groups**, add color groups: `path:"Old Testament"` (one color), `path:"New Testament"` (another), `path:Hubs`, and `file:"Jesus Christ"` (make Him gold).
- Turn up **link distance** and **repel force** to spread the constellation out.
- Use the filter `tag:#hub` off/on to compare structural links vs. book-to-book links.

## What's in Each Book Note

- **Original language** — the Hebrew/Greek title and what it means
- **Christ in this book** — every book, no exceptions, points to the Cross and the Resurrection
- **Key Verse** — full KJV text
- **Take It Home** — one direct application. Scripture is for obeying, not just admiring (James 1:22)
- **Connections** — the books it quotes or is quoted by
- **Full text** — every chapter, every verse, KJV
