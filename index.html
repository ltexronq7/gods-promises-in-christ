Built to serve the Church

Use the promise index for personal study, discipleship, teaching preparation, small groups, handouts, or building new tools.

Study responsibly. Promise summaries, categories, and “Christ in this book” introductions are editorial study aids, not replacements for reading Scripture in context. Distinguish promises addressed to particular people or covenants from universal commands and assurances. Test every teaching from the biblical text.
